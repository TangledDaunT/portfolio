Case Studies

LegalLawAdvisor — RAG Pipeline for Legal Research

Problem: A High Court lawyer needed to search 5.4M+ legal documents for relevant judgments and statutes.

Solution: Built a RAG pipeline with Qdrant vector database, semantic search, and LLM explanation layer.

Results: Sub-400ms retrieval time, 5.4M+ documents indexed, production deployment serving real users.

Tech: Python, FastAPI, Qdrant, LangChain, OpenAI

Voice Journal — Offline-First AI Voice Assistant

Problem: Needed a local, always-on voice journal that logs to Obsidian without cloud dependencies.

Solution: Built a Python application with local speech recognition, CPU-only inference, and Obsidian integration.

Results: Fully offline, CPU-only, always-on voice capture with structured logging.

Tech: Python, Whisper, Obsidian API, Local LLM

MindBridge — Mental Health SaaS

Problem: Therapists needed a tool to track patient emotional patterns and prepare session briefs.

Solution: Built a SaaS app with emotion tracking, pattern analysis, and automated session brief generation.

Results: Streamlined therapist workflow, improved patient care through data-driven insights.

Tech: React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, OpenAI
